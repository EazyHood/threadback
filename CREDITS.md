## Earlier work and the new contribution

[Benchkeep](https://github.com/EazyHood/benchkeep), a previous project associated with the entrant, preserves a photograph, an exact point, and a next move for unfinished craft work. It predates Taskforge and is a conceptual antecedent. The pause-and-return idea is therefore not presented as newly invented for this event.

Threadback uses a new codebase and a new academic workflow: exact study anchor, previous decision, next action, and explicit behavior for returning, replanning, remaining blocked, finishing, and reopening. The implementation record declares **no reuse of Benchkeep source code, UI assets, screenshots, or video**. The conceptual relationship is disclosed even though the implementation is separate; a new codebase does not make the general pause-and-return idea new.
